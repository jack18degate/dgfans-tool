Risks & important notes

Risks to consider. Fees do not guarantee a profit: market losses and underperformance relative to holding the assets (impermanent loss) may exceed the fees earned. Outside the range, a position consists of a single token and earns no new fees. Smart contract, liquidity, stablecoin, and issuer risks remain. RFQ trades do not automatically generate fees for Turbo Range liquidity providers. Stock tokens provide economic exposure and are not equivalent to direct share ownership. Check access requirements and suitability. Animations are illustrative: 0.25% is an example fee, not an applicable rate or forecast; NVDA/USDC percentages represent shares of position value, excluding fees.
